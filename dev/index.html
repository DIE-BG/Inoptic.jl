Inflation Optimal Combination Functions

The Inoptic package provides functions to compute optimal inflation measures based on the methodologies developed by the DIE-BG research group.

Inoptic.Inoptic — Module
Inoptic.jl

A Julia package to save, load the optimal inflation measures based on the methodologies developed by the DIE-BG research group.
